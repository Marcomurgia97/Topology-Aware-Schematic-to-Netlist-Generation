**PHASE 1: Global Identification and Verification of Nets**
The primary labeled nets in the schematic are **Vin**, **Vout**, **CK**, and **0** (ground).
An inspection of the circuit nodes reveals:
- The node connecting the input terminal and the left terminal of the transistor M1 is directly labeled **Vin**.
- The node connecting the gate of the transistor M1 is directly labeled **CK**.
- The node connecting the right terminal of transistor M1, the top plate of capacitor CH, and the output terminal is directly labeled **Vout**.
- The bottom plate of capacitor CH is connected to the ground symbol, designated as net **0**.
Since all nodes are directly connected to labeled terminals or ground, no new net names need to be assigned.
The final net inventory is: **Vin**, **Vout**, **CK**, **0**.

**PHASE 2: Description of Components and Connections**

- **Component M1:** This is an N-channel MOSFET (NMOS).
    - **Analysis:**
        - Pin identification logic: The MOSFET symbol consists of a Gate terminal separated from a channel. On the channel side, there are two terminals. The terminal marked with an outward-pointing arrow is the Source, and the remaining terminal is the Drain.
        - Pin Mapping: The single terminal on the top side is the **Gate**. Of the two terminals on the bottom side, the one on the right has the arrow, identifying it as the **Source**. The terminal on the left without the arrow is the **Drain**.
    - **Final Connections:**
        - Its **gate** is connected to the **CK** net.
        - Its **drain** is connected to the **Vin** net.
        - Its **source** is connected to the **Vout** net.

- **Component CH:** This is a capacitor.
    - **Final Connections:**
        - One plate is connected to the **Vout** net.
        - The other plate is connected to the **0** (ground) net.
