The primary labeled nets are $V_{in}$, $V_{out}$, $V_{DD}$, and $V_b$.
An unlabeled node connecting the gate of transistor $M_1$ and one side of resistor $R_G$ was identified. A check confirms it is NOT directly connected to any other labeled net (it is separated from the $V_b$ terminal by the resistor component $R_G$). Therefore, it is assigned the new name `n1`.

- **Component M1:** This is an NMOS transistor.
    - **Analysis:**
        - Pin identification logic: A MOSFET symbol has one pin on one side (Gate) and two on the other. The pin with the outward-pointing arrow is the Source. The remaining pin is the Drain.

        - Its **source** is connected to the $V_{in}$ net.
        - Its **drain** is connected to the $V_{out}$ net.

    - **Final Connections:**
