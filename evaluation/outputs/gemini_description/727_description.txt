**PHASE 1: Global Identification and Verification of Nets**

The primary labeled nets are **VDD**, **Vin**, **Vout**, and **0** (ground).
- **VDD**: The main positive power supply rail running horizontally across the top.
- **Vin**: The input terminal on the left of the amplifier stage.
- **Vout**: The output terminal on the right of the amplifier stage.
- **0**: The ground reference, indicated by multiple ground symbols at the bottom of the schematic.

Several important unlabeled nodes connecting multiple components were identified and verified to be distinct from the labeled nets:
- **n1**: A bias voltage node connecting the drain and gate of Mb1, the drain of Mb3, and the gates of Mb2 and M11.
- **n2**: A bias voltage node connecting the drain and gate of Mb4, the gate of Mb3, and the drain of Mb2.
- **n3**: A node connecting the source of Mb4 to the bottom of resistor RS.
- **n4**: An intermediate node in the compensation network connecting resistor RZ to capacitor Cc.

The final net inventory is: **VDD, Vin, Vout, 0, n1, n2, n3, n4**.
