**PHASE 1: Global Identification and Verification of Nets**

1.  **List Labeled Nets:**
    -   **VPP**: The positive power supply rail indicated by the upward arrows at the top.
    -   **vi1**: The input signal net on the left side, connected to the positive terminal of the voltage source labeled $v_{i1}$.
    -   **vi2**: The input signal net on the right side, connected to the positive terminal of the voltage source labeled $v_{i2}$.
    -   **vO1**: The output net on the left side, taken at the plate of the left vacuum tube.
    -   **vO2**: The output net on the right side, taken at the plate of the right vacuum tube.
    -   **0**: The ground reference, connected to the negative terminals of the input sources and the bottom of resistor $R_T$.

2.  **Identify and Verify Unlabeled Nodes:**
    -   **Left Grid Node:** The junction connecting the left input capacitor, the grid of the left tube, and the top of the left resistor $R_G$. This is a distinct node not connected to any labeled net. It is assigned the name **n1**.
    -   **Right Grid Node:** The junction connecting the right input capacitor, the grid of the right tube, and the top of the right resistor $R_G$. This is a distinct node. It is assigned the name **n2**.
    -   **Cathode Coupling Node:** The junction connecting the cathode of the left tube, the cathode of the right tube, and the top of resistor $R'_K$. This is a distinct node. It is assigned the name **n3**.
    -   **Bias Junction Node:** The node connecting the bottom of the left resistor $R_G$, the bottom of the right resistor $R_G$, the bottom of resistor $R'_K$, and the top of resistor $R_T$. This is a distinct node. It is assigned the name **n4**.

3.  **Final Net Inventory:** VPP, vi1, vi2, vO1, vO2, 0, n1, n2, n3, n4.
