**PHASE 1: Global Identification and Verification of Nets**

1.  **List Labeled Nets:**
    The schematic defines a two-terminal port on the right side labeled with impedance **Z1**. The top terminal of this port serves as the first primary net, which we will identify as **Net_Top_Terminal**. The bottom terminal of this port serves as the second primary net, identified as **Net_Bottom_Terminal**.

2.  **Identify and Verify Unlabeled Nodes:**
    An inspection of the circuit reveals an internal junction where three components meet: the left side of the inductor **L**, the left side of resistor **R1**, and the top side of resistor **R2**.
    -   **Verification Check:** Is this node directly connected to **Net_Top_Terminal**? No, it is separated by components L and R1. Is it connected to **Net_Bottom_Terminal**? No, it is separated by resistor R2.
    -   **Result:** Since this node is electrically distinct from the terminal nets, it is assigned the new unique name **n1**.

3.  **Final Net Inventory:**
    The complete list of verified nets in the circuit is: **Net_Top_Terminal**, **Net_Bottom_Terminal**, and **n1**.
