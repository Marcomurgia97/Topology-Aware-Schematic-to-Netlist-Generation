**PHASE 1: Global Identification and Verification of Nets**

1.  **List Labeled Nets:**
    -   **VDD**: The positive power supply rail at the top.
    -   **X**: The node labeled on the left branch.
    -   **Y**: The node labeled on the right branch.
    -   **0**: The ground reference, indicated by three ground symbols at the bottom.

2.  **Identify and Verify Unlabeled Nodes:**
    -   **Gate/Bias Node:** There is a node connecting the Gate of transistor M2, the Gate of transistor M1, and the top of resistor R2. A visible wire with a connection dot links this junction directly to the node labeled **X** (which is also the Drain of M2). Therefore, this entire interconnected region (Drain M2, Gate M2, Gate M1, Top R2, Bottom Left R1) is a single electrical point. It is identified as net **X**.
    -   **Ground Connections:** The Source of M2, the Source of M1, and the bottom of R2 each connect directly to ground symbols. These are all part of net **0**.
    -   **Right Branch Node:** The connection between the Right R1 and the Drain of M1 is labeled **Y**.

3.  **Final Net Inventory:**
    -   VDD, X, Y, 0.

    -   **Analysis:**
        -   **Pin Identification Logic:** The symbol is oriented with the Gate facing inwards to the right. The single pin on the right side is the **Gate**. On the left side, there are two pins: the bottom pin has an arrow pointing outward, identifying it as the **Source**. The remaining top pin on the left is the **Drain**.
        -   **Pin Mapping:** Based on the schematic lines, the Gate connects to the central wire (net X). The Source connects to ground (net 0). The Drain connects to the node X.
    -   **Final Connections:**

        -   Its **drain** is connected to the **X** net (effectively diode-connecting the transistor).
