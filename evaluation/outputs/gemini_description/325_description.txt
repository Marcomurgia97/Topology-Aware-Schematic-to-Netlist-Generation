**PHASE 1: Global Identification and Verification of Nets**

The primary labeled nets in the schematic are **VDD** (the positive supply rail), **X**, **Y**, and **0** (ground, indicated by the ground symbols).

I have analyzed the schematic for unlabeled nodes:
1.  **M2 Gate/Drain Connection:** The node connecting the drain of transistor M2, the gate of transistor M2, the gate of transistor M1, and the bottom of the left resistor R1 is explicitly labeled **X**. A check confirms all these points share the same electrical potential.
2.  **M1 Source Connection:** There is a node connecting the source of transistor M1 and the top of resistor R2. This node is not directly connected to any labeled net (VDD, X, Y, or 0). Therefore, it is assigned the new unique name **n1**.
3.  **M2 Source Connection:** The source of transistor M2 is directly connected to the ground symbol. This is part of net **0**.

The final net inventory is: **VDD, 0, X, Y, n1**.
