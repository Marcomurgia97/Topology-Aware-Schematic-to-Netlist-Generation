**PHASE 1: Global Identification and Verification of Nets**
The primary labeled nets in the schematic are **VDD**, **Vin**, **Vout**, **Vb**, and **0** (ground).
Three distinct unlabeled nodes connecting multiple components were identified and verified:
1.  The node connecting the drain of transistor M1 and the source of transistor M2. This node is not directly connected to any labeled net. It is assigned the unique name **n1**.
2.  The node connecting the gate of transistor M2, the right side of resistor R1, and the left plate of capacitor C1. This node is assigned the unique name **n2**.
3.  The node connecting the gate of transistor M3, the left side of resistor R2, and the right plate of capacitor C2. This node is assigned the unique name **n3**.
The final net inventory is: **VDD**, **Vin**, **Vout**, **Vb**, **0**, **n1**, **n2**, **n3**.
