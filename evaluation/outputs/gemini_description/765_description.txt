**PHASE 1: Global Identification and Verification of Nets**

1.  **List Labeled Nets:**
    -   **VDD**: The positive power supply rail at the top.
    -   **0**: The ground reference indicated by the ground symbols at the bottom (connected to M1 source and Rs).

2.  **Identify and Verify Unlabeled Nodes:**
    -   **Node 1:** A junction connecting the Drain of M4, the Drain of M1, the Gate of M1, the Gate of M2, and the Source of M5. A check confirms this node is not directly connected to VDD or Ground. This is a distinct net.
    -   **Node 2:** A junction connecting the Drain of M3, the Drain of M2, the Gate of M3, the Gate of M4, the Drain of M5, and the Gate of M5. A check confirms this node is not directly connected to VDD or Ground. This is a distinct net.
    -   **Node 3:** A junction connecting the Source of M2 and the resistor Rs. This is not connected directly to any other net. This is a distinct net.

3.  **Assign Names and Finalize Inventory:**
    -   **n1**: The common node for the left branch output and NMOS gates.
    -   **n2**: The common node for the right branch output and PMOS gates.
    -   **n3**: The source degeneration node for M2.
    -   **Final Net Inventory:** VDD, 0, n1, n2, n3.
