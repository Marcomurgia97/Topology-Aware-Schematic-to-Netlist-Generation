    *   **X**: A primary labeled node connecting the resistor R1, the drain of M2, the gates of both transistors, and the negative terminal of capacitor C1.
    *   **Y**: A primary labeled node connecting the drain of M1 and the positive terminal of capacitor C1.

2.  **Identify and Verify Unlabeled Nodes:**
    *   The connections involving the gates of M1 and M2, the drain of M2, and the resistor R1 are all interconnected via visible junction dots. Specifically, a dot connects the vertical wire from the drain of M2 to the horizontal wire connecting the gates. Another dot connects the resistor R1 to the drain of M2 and the capacitor C1. Therefore, all these points form a single electrical net, which is already identified as **X**.

3.  **Final Net Inventory:**
    *   The verified nets are: **VDD**, **0**, **X**, and **Y**.

    -   **Final Connections:**
        -   One terminal is connected to the **VDD** net.
        -   The other terminal is connected to the **X** net.

-   **Component M2:** This is an N-channel MOSFET (NMOS).
    -   **Analysis:**
        -   Pin identification logic: The MOSFET symbol has a single pin on the left side (Gate) and two pins on the right side. The pin with the arrow pointing outward is the Source. The remaining pin is the Drain.
        -   Pin Mapping: The Gate is connected to the horizontal line joined to the drain (diode connection). The Source connects to ground. The Drain connects to the resistor R1.
    -   **Final Connections:**
        -   Its **gate** is connected to the **X** net.
        -   Its **drain** is connected to the **X** net.
        -   Its **source** is connected to the **0** (ground) net.

-   **Component M1:** This is an N-channel MOSFET (NMOS).
    -   **Analysis:**
        -   Pin identification logic: The MOSFET symbol has a single pin on the left side (Gate) and two pins on the right side. The pin with the arrow pointing outward is the Source. The remaining pin is the Drain.

        -   Its **drain** is connected to the **Y** net.

    -   **Analysis:**
