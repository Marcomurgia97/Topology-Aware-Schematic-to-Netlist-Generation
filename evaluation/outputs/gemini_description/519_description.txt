**PHASE 1: Global Identification and Verification of Nets**
The primary labeled nets identified in the schematic are VDD, Vin, Vout, and 0 (ground).
An unlabeled node was identified connecting the gate of transistor M1, the bottom terminal of the upper resistor R1, and the top terminal of the lower resistor R1. A verification check confirms this node is NOT directly connected to any other labeled net (it is separated from Vout by the upper resistor and from ground by the lower resistor). Therefore, it is assigned the new unique name `n1`.
The final net inventory is: VDD, Vin, Vout, 0, n1.

**PHASE 2: Description of Components and Connections**
- **Component M1:** This is an N-channel MOSFET.
    - **Analysis:**
        - Pin identification logic: A MOSFET symbol has one pin on one side and two on the other. The single pin on its own side is the Gate. Of the two pins on the other side, the one with an arrow is the Source. The remaining pin is the Drain.
        - Pin Mapping: In this specific symbol, the Gate is on the right side and connects to `n1`. On the left side, the bottom pin has an arrow pointing outward, identifying it as the Source; it connects to `Vin`. The top pin on the left is the Drain; it connects to `Vout`.
    - **Final Connections:**
        - Its **gate** is connected to the 'n1' node.
        - Its **source** is connected to the 'Vin' node.
        - Its **drain** is connected to the 'Vout' node.

- **Component RD:** This is a resistor acting as the drain load.
    - **Final Connections:**
        - One terminal is connected to the 'VDD' node.
        - The other terminal is connected to the 'Vout' node.

- **Component R1 (Upper):** This is the upper resistor in the feedback divider network.
    - **Final Connections:**
        - One terminal is connected to the 'Vout' node.
        - The other terminal is connected to the 'n1' node.

- **Component R1 (Lower):** This is the lower resistor in the feedback divider network.
    - **Final Connections:**
        - One terminal is connected to the 'n1' node.
        - The other terminal is connected to the '0' (ground) node.
